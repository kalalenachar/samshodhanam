श्रीत्रयीविद्यागुरुकुलम्

Sri Trayee Vidya Gurukulam

Samshodhana Mandiram is the research, manuscript digitization, and AI training data wing of Sri Trayee Vidya Gurukulam. The Gurukulam is dedicated to preserving authentic Vedic learning, manuscript conservation, and modern digital technology to safeguard Indian Knowledge Systems.

Ecosystem Features

What Samshodhana Mandiram delivers to scholars, researchers, and AI builders

Sanskrit Digital Library

Complete manuscript cataloging with page-level text extraction, OCR proofreading, and fast search.

AI Training Data & Datasets

High-precision OCR correction, Sandhi splitting, and structured linguistic corpora for training AI models.

Semantic Vector Search

Contextual search powered by vector embeddings and AI to locate deep philosophical concepts.

Play & Vocabulary Suite

Interactive vocabulary games including Word Builder, Synonym Match, and Word Search puzzle.

Katha & Scholarly Articles

Illustrated stories with audio narration, interactive word breakdowns, and curated academic research.

Quiz & Analytics

Real-time quizzes, scoring, detailed explanations, and progress tracking for Sanskrit learners.

Our Core Philosophy

Guided by three foundational pillars of digital Sanskrit preservation

संरक्षणम्

Preservation

Digitizing ancient manuscripts, palm-leaf folios, and out-of-print classical texts to safeguard timeless wisdom for future generations.

अनुसन्धानम्

Research & Innovation

Fusing traditional Paninian grammar with computational NLP, AI proofreading, and semantic search across authentic Shastras.

संवर्धनम्

Open Access & Growth

Championing open source software, open academic data, and unrestricted digital access, ensuring timeless Sanskrit wisdom is preserved and shared universally without commercial gatekeeping.

मुक्तस्रोतः मुक्तांशश्च • Open Source & Open Data

Committed to Open Source Software & Open Sanskrit Data

We believe authentic Sanskrit heritage belongs to all humanity. Samshodhana Mandiram builds transparent, community-accessible tools and provides open academic data to power next-generation Indic computing and research.

मुक्तस्रोत-तन्त्रांशः

Open Source Codebase

Our platform codebase, Sanskrit NLP processors, OCR proofreading workflows, and interactive linguistic engines are developed under an open-source ethos for the global academic and developer community.

  • AIOC 2026 Public Release: Official repository unveiling and research paper presentation scheduled for October 31, 2026 at the 52nd All India Oriental Conference.
  • Transparent NLP Architectures: Sandhi splitters, Samasa disambiguation, phonetic transliterators, and grammar engines built for peer review and community extension.
  • Community Collaboration: Inviting developers, researchers, and Indic AI enthusiasts to contribute code, report issues, and build derivative tools.
Public Repo (AIOC 2026)
विवृत्त-दत्तांशः

Open Sanskrit Data & Corpora

Preserving classical manuscripts is meaningless if locked behind commercial paywalls. We liberate digitized texts, verified transcriptions, and vector datasets for public scholarly benefit.

  • Unrestricted Academic Access: Digitized manuscripts, OCR page layers, and proofread Sanskrit Shastras accessible for non-commercial scholarly research.
  • AI & LLM-Ready Corpora: Clean, tokenized Sanskrit text chunks and high-dimensional semantic vector embeddings shared to train Indic language models.
  • Zero Paywalls or Gatekeeping: Faithful to the sacred Gurukula principle of Vidya Dana — authentic knowledge preserved and gifted freely to all seekers.
