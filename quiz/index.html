🧠 QuizMaster — Instructions

General Instructions

Please read carefully

1. Total duration of the examination depends on the number of questions selected.

2. The clock will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination.

3. The Questions Palette status symbols:

1
Not visited yet.
2
Not answered.
3
Answered.
4
Marked for review.
5
Answered & Marked for Review (will be evaluated).

Answering a Question

  1. To select your answer, click on the button of one of the options.
  2. To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear Response button.
  3. To save your answer, you MUST click on the Save & Next button.
  4. To mark the question for review, click on the Mark for Review & Next button.
